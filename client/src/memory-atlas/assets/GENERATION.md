# Ember Veil · 原创星云纹理

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成。用户提供的四张星云截图仅用于视觉方向参考，没有采用截图中的界面或署名作为网页素材。

文件：`ember-veil-nebula.png`。这是一张原创合成星云纹理，不是实际望远镜观测数据。网页将它作为深空景观层，另叠加 WebGL 实时星群、体积云雾和记忆节点。

## 生成提示词

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: an original astronomical nebula texture for an interactive WebGL memory observatory, no UI. Create a breathtaking, physically plausible deep-sky astrophotography of an expansive emission nebula, a horizontal panoramic composition about 16:10. Inspired by the fine filigree of Hubble narrowband nebula imagery: a deep cobalt-blue and ice-blue ionized hollow through the middle-left, luminous coral-red, burnt copper and muted pink cloud walls flowing diagonally across the middle and curling irregularly into the lower-right, dark branching interstellar dust lanes and detailed opaque dusty pillars partly silhouetted against blue light. Rich tiny billowing wisps, translucent fractal filaments, thin amber lit edges, realistic subtle grain and fine natural detail, deep three-dimensional gas layers. The composition should feel like looking deep into open space, with asymmetric broken cloud ridges and an irregular open central cavity, not an object placed on a black backdrop. Edges transition into dark inky black space, with enough deep shadow for interface readability. The colored nebulosity must fill most of the frame horizontally, detailed but with restrained exposure, luminous luminous blue heart yet no blown-out white areas. Mostly starless cloud texture; the application will add live stars, so do not draw huge or bright stars, star spikes or bokeh. Natural astronomical photograph, not a smooth CGI gradient or fantasy painting. No spherical boundary, no spherical star cluster, no galaxy spiral, no concentric rings, no planet, no lens flare, no spaceship, no text, no labels, no watermark, no mobile phone borders, no screenshot chrome. Landscape image, original composition, cinematic depth and photographic fine structure.
